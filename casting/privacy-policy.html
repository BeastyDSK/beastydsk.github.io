Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CastControl application (the "Application") created by Krishnaprasanth ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our"). This service is provided "AS IS".

1. Information We Process

The Application is a local screen-casting utility. Most casting operations run on your device and local network.

The Application may process the following categories of information:

  • Device and session data: device model, Android version, app version, session state, codec and transport diagnostics.
  • Network discovery data: nearby display discovery details (for example Wi-Fi Direct peer metadata needed for connection flow).
  • Casting configuration: selected resolution, frame rate, bitrate, latency mode, transport mode, and other local settings.
  • Purchase entitlement state: ad-removal and premium entitlement status from Google Play Billing (we do not receive payment card details).
  • Support communication: if you contact us manually, we may process the content you send to provide support.

2. Permissions and Why They Are Needed

  • Nearby devices / location-related access (Android-dependent): used to discover compatible displays.
  • Screen capture permission: required only when you start casting.
  • Audio capture permission: used to include system audio when supported by your device and receiver.

3. How We Use Data

  • Discover nearby displays and establish casting sessions.
  • Apply your selected quality and transport settings.
  • Show diagnostics and session stats in the app UI.
  • Validate and restore paid feature entitlements through Google Play Billing.
  • Improve app reliability through local diagnostics and user-reported issues.

4. Local Processing and Storage

CastControl performs core casting and session handling locally on your device. App preferences and local session-related state are stored on-device.

5. Network and Service Providers

The Application may communicate with:

  • Google Play Billing for purchase flow and entitlement restoration.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Local network devices (such as casting receivers) required for casting setup and streaming.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) used to deliver in-app ads for ad-supported access.

6. Advertising

If you use an ad-supported experience, ads may be served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (including advertising identifiers), approximate location signals, and app interaction data to deliver, measure, and improve ads.

You can manage ad personalization in your Google account and device privacy settings. You can stop ad delivery in this app by purchasing ad removal where offered.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with providers needed for app operation (for example Google Play Billing and Google AdMob).

8. Retention and Deletion

  • Local app data: remove by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Purchase records: handled by Google Play.
  • Support data: retained only as needed for support and legal compliance.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
