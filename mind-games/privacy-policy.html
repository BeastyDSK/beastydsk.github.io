Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026 · Effective: June 7, 2026 Version 1.1

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mind Games application (the "Application") created by Krishnaprasanth ("Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our"), an individual operating as a sole proprietor based in Tamil Nadu, India. This service is provided "AS IS".

Mind Games is a daily puzzle application offering five short puzzles per day (Word, Sudoku, Math Sprint, Memory Tiles, Logic Riddle) with a local day-streak counter. The Application does not require account sign-up, and we operate no backend servers that store your personal data.

1. Information We Process

The Application may process the following categories of information:

  • Gameplay data (stored only on your device): puzzle completions, scores, best results, and your day streak. Never transmitted to our servers.
  • App preferences (stored only on your device): your premium status, ad preference, and notification / sound / haptic settings.
  • Advertising identifiers and signals: Google Advertising ID, IP address, coarse / approximate location inferred from IP, and ad interaction data, processed by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads. Subject to your consent choice (see Section 4).
  • Purchase state: the result of the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, received from Google Play Billing. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google and are not visible to us.
  • Consent state: your GDPR / CCPA consent choice, stored only on your device by the consent management library.
  • Analytics events (processed by Firebase Analytics): anonymized in-app events such as puzzle started, puzzle completed, streak updated, hint used, ad watched, and in-app purchase events. Firebase also automatically collects device model, OS version, app version, coarse country, language, session duration, and first-open timestamp — all anonymized and aggregated. Collection is enabled or disabled based on your consent choice (see Section 4).
  • Crash and stability reports (processed by Firebase Crashlytics): if the Application crashes or produces an ANR, Crashlytics automatically captures a stack trace along with device model, OS version, app version, and the state of the Application at the time of the crash. No personally identifiable information is intentionally included in these reports. Collection is enabled or disabled based on your consent choice (see Section 4).

2. How We Use Data

  • Run daily puzzles, track your local day streak, and display your puzzle history.
  • Show two daily local reminder notifications (a morning "puzzles ready" reminder and an evening "streak at risk" reminder). Notifications are generated on your device and are not delivered through any push service.
  • Show ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video) when ads are enabled for your install.
  • Process the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase through Google Play Billing and remember the result on your device.
  • Check for app updates and surface the Play Store review prompt through Google Play Core services.
  • Collect anonymized usage analytics through Firebase Analytics to understand how the Application is used and improve it over time (subject to your consent choice).
  • Collect anonymized crash and stability reports through Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix bugs (subject to your consent choice).

7. Notifications

The Application shows two local reminder notifications per day: a morning "puzzles ready" reminder and an evening "streak at risk" reminder if you have not completed today's puzzles. Notifications are generated on your device and are not delivered through any push service. On Android 13 and later, the Application asks for the notification permission on first launch. You may decline or revoke it at any time from your device's Settings.

8. Local Data and Backups

All gameplay data and preferences are stored on your device. Your premium purchase status is intentionally excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so that paid features are not restored on an unrelated install or device.

Other local data (such as your streak and puzzle history) follows the standard Android backup behaviour and may be restored from your Google account if you reinstall the Application while signed in to the same Google account. To delete this backed-up data, manage app backups from your Google account at https://drive.google.com/drive/backups.

9. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Account registration data — the Application does not require signup.
  • Precise GPS location (we do not request the location permission). Note: as disclosed in Sections 1 and 5, AdMob may infer your approximate location from your IP address.
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, or camera content.
  • Payment card or banking credentials — these are handled entirely by Google Play Billing.
  • Cloud-stored leaderboards, social features, or user-generated content — the Application has none.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising other than as described in Sections 4 and 5. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed in Section 5, strictly to operate the features they support (advertising, billing, updates, and reviews).
