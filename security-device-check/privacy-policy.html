Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Device Security Check application (hereby referred to as the "Application") created by Krishnaprasanth (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our"). This service is provided "AS IS".

1. Information We Process

The Application is a device security diagnostic tool. Most analysis is performed locally on your device.

The Application may process the following information:

  • Device information such as device model, Android version, and basic system configuration.
  • Installed application metadata, such as package names, installer source information, and declared permissions.
  • Security diagnostic results generated by scans performed within the Application.
  • Play Integrity API responses and related device integrity signals, where supported.

2. App Visibility and Installed Apps Access

The Application uses the QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission to access the list of applications installed on your device. This access is required for the core functionality of the Application.

The Application uses installed app visibility data to:

  • Identify apps installed from unknown, unofficial, or untrusted sources.
  • Analyze declared app permissions and related metadata for potential security or privacy risks.
  • Help detect apps that may require review based on risk signals shown inside the Application.

Installed app visibility data is processed locally on your device and is not used for advertising, profiling, or unrelated analytics purposes.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect or store the following through the Application:

  • Your contacts, messages, photos, videos, or other personal files.
  • Precise GPS location data.
  • Passwords, payment card information, or banking details.
  • User-created account data, because the Application does not require account registration.

4. How the Application Uses Data

The Application uses processed information to:

  • Analyze device security posture and app risk signals.
  • Display security findings, alerts, and explanations within the Application.
  • Generate local diagnostic reports, including PDF exports where available.

5. Local Processing and Storage

Security scans, app analysis, and most diagnostic processing occur locally on your device. Scan results and generated reports are generally stored locally unless a feature explicitly requires interaction with a third-party service.

6. Background Scanning

If you enable periodic background scanning, the Application may perform scheduled checks on your device to identify newly installed apps or changes relevant to security analysis. These scans are intended to run on-device.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by third-party providers only to the extent necessary for supported integrity checks and billing functionality.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Purchase records: In-app purchase records are handled through Google Play.
